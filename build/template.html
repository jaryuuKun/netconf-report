India data centresGo-to-market research for network configuration (NETCONF) tooling

A facility is a data centre building where companies rent space for their routers and switches. The more networks installed in a facility, the more equipment there is to configure, which makes it a stronger prospect for NETCONF tooling. NETCONF is the standard protocol for configuring that equipment automatically instead of by hand. The bar below splits every installed network across the three facility sizes used in this report.

Networks

A network is a company, such as an internet service provider, a content provider or a carrier, that runs its own routers and switches. Each one is a potential buyer in its own right, separate from the facility operator. Every network has an ASN, the unique number it uses to identify itself on the internet.

Internet exchanges

An internet exchange is a shared switch where many networks connect directly to swap traffic with each other, an arrangement called peering. Each connected network plugs into a port on that switch, and port speed is measured in megabits or gigabits per second. Exchange operators set up and change those connections constantly, so they carry a heavy configuration workload.

Which exchanges are largest?i

Each bubble is one exchange: networks connected against total port capacity. Click a bubble to open the exchange.

What port speeds do networks buy?

The number of operational ports at each speed. 10G means 10 gigabits per second. Ports with no recorded speed, and ports marked not operational, are left out.

NIXI

The National Internet Exchange of India (NIXI) is the government-backed internet exchange. It runs connection points, called NOCs (network operations centres), in cities across the country where local internet service providers plug in. It matters here for two reasons: NIXI is one organisation running many sites, so one conversation covers all of them, and its ISP lists include regional ISPs that do not appear anywhere in the facility data.

How many of NIXI's ISPs are new to us?i

Every ISP connected at NIXI, checked against the facility and exchange data by ASN. Each ISP falls into the first group it fits. Anything outside the first group would be missed by the facility list alone. Click a group to filter the page.

    A note on the map

    The state boundaries on the maps are simplified public data, drawn for visual reference only. They are not survey-grade and should not be used to read a border. A city bubble sits at the average position of that city's facilities that have coordinates in the data; facilities in a city where no facility has coordinates stay in every chart, table and count, and are listed under each map.
